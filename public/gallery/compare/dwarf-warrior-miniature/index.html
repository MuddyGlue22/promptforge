Dwarf Warrior Miniature: one prompt, two models

The same dwarf with the Painted miniature style, which always asks for a full-body figure on its round base. One prompt, sent unchanged to two image models on OpenMov, and two very different takes on a painted mini.

The prompt

Generate an image.

SUBJECT
A middle-aged, stocky male dwarf, short and broad-shouldered, with a sturdy frame and a thick, braided beard, portrayed as a warrior, in practical, battle-worn gear, with visible scars and a well-used weapon. A fantasy tabletop RPG character.

APPEARANCE
hair: shaved, eyes: brown, skin: tan, expression: determined.

GEAR
wearing plate, carrying a massive two-handed greataxe with a long haft and a broad, heavy blade, accessories: cloak, pauldron, gauntlets, tabard.

SCENE
Framing: full-body shot, the entire figure visible from head to toe, standing on its round base. Pose: relaxed stance. Setting: ruined city. Lighting: warm firelight.

STYLE
A hand-painted tabletop miniature figure on a round base, photographed close up, with fine brushwork, crisp highlights, and the setting shown as a small painted diorama.

REQUIREMENTS
A single character, clearly the focus of the image. Correct anatomy with natural hands. No text, lettering, logos, brand marks, captions, watermarks, signatures, or borders.
