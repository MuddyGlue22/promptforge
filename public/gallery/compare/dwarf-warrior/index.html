One prompt from the builder, using the new Photorealistic style, sent unchanged to four image models on OpenMov. Same words, same settings, four quite different dwarves. Each image is shown exactly as it came out.

The prompt

Generate an image.

SUBJECT
A middle-aged, stocky male dwarf, short and broad-shouldered, with a sturdy frame and a thick, braided beard, portrayed as a warrior, in practical, battle-worn gear, with visible scars and a well-used weapon. A fantasy tabletop RPG character.

APPEARANCE
hair: shaved, eyes: brown, skin: tan, expression: determined.

GEAR
wearing plate, carrying greataxe, accessories: cloak, pauldron, gauntlets, tabard.

SCENE
Framing: three-quarter shot, framed from the knees up. Pose: relaxed stance. Setting: ruined city. Lighting: warm firelight.

STYLE
Photorealistic, like a still from a high-budget fantasy film: real skin and material textures, practical costume and props, and a shallow depth of field.

REQUIREMENTS
A single character, clearly the focus of the image. Correct anatomy with natural hands. No text, lettering, logos, brand marks, captions, watermarks, signatures, or borders.
