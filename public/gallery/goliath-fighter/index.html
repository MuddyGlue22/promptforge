How it turned out

Third attempt. In an empty mountain pass (Seedream) nothing showed his seven-to-eight-foot height, so we moved him to a tavern, where there are people and doorways to compare him with. Seedream still left the tavern empty, but Nano Banana filled it with customers despite the prompt asking for a single character, and he clearly towers over them. The greataxe is finally right too: long-hafted and double-bladed. The stone-like patches on his skin are fainter than in Seedream's versions, showing mainly on his head.
